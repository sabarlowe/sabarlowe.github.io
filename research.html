Research Statement

My research interests span a variety of areas including visualization, data mining, bioinformatics, HCI, and serious game development. My primary interest is searching for visualization techniques that aid in understanding large, multidimensional datasets. Challenges include finding an automatic analysis approach which meets user goals for revealing interesting data features and then finding methods to coherently tie the features together so that visualizations are efficient and easily interpreted. My dissertation work concentrated on integrating novel visualizations, interactions, and signal processing for revealing hidden features in a protein flexibility model. Specifically, I applied wavelet analysis to protein flexibility matrices for characterizing both local and global characteristics within a highly interactive visualization framework. The framework was further extended to included subspace similarity analysis. Current work focuses on the interactive visualization of uncertainty in multiple protein sequence alignments so that scientists can better understand the quality of underlying algorithms. (See Protein Subspace Exploration, WaveMap, and Microarray Data Reduction below.)

I am also interested in game development. This is a wide field but I am mostly concerned with finding what methodologies and techniques in game design can be applied to other domains. This includes finding areas where game environments can make learning, building, or investigation more effective and efficient. Furthermore, appropriate methods of storytelling, presentation, and flow for an individual domain need to be defined. Identifying the domains suitable for applying game technologies and improving content delivery will help expedite the effects of instructional or investigative efforts.

Selected Projects

Prototype for the North Carolina Board of Science, Technology and Innovation
/ Department of Commerce (2023) What is the distribution of data literacy secondary
school pathways and higher education certificate and degree programs by NC county and tier?
Prototype can be found here.

Vocabulo (2021) Mobile application for real-time language acquisition. Utilizes machine learning and a
scanvenger hunt format. Supports text and audio translation of objects recognized
by the application. Objects to be found during the scavenger hunt can be defined by
the user or by a target word list. Implemented by two capstone students under my direction.
A demo video can be found here.

SubVis (2017) Interactive, web-based platform written in R and JavaScript for facilitating a visual
analytics approach to exploring the varying results from applying different substitution
matrices in pairwise sequence alignment. Consists of an overview, detail view, and
search view. SubVis source code is posted on CRAN and two demo videos can be found
here.

Protein Subspace Exploration (2013) Video (Windows Media Video with audio)
Interactive, visual platform for investigating small groups of residues in protein flexibility matrices so
global protein behavior can be linked to small, but coordinated, residue actions.

WaveMap (2011) Video (Windows Media Video with audio)
Interactive, visual analytics application used to examine protein flexibility correlation plots. This project
applied wavelet analysis to reveal features in protein flexibility matrices that are otherwise difficult to
find, analyze, and compare. Resulting features and novel interactions were combined to allow an effective
pipeline progressing from an overview of the dataset, to matrix level analysis with a coordinated lens
and feature snapping, and finally to coordinated residue-level evaluation.

Microarray Data Reduction (2005)
Implemented Haar lifting wavelet in MATLAB for microarray data reduction.
This research found that clustering results did not change after greatly reducing the data.
